# English Version

Yesterday (2026-06-09), Anthropic publicly launched **Claude Fable 5** — the first publicly available model with the same vulnerability-discovery capability that, two months ago, was kept behind a tightly controlled "defense-only" guest list.

## What actually changed

- The ability to find software security holes at industrial speed used to be locked up. Now it sits inside every paid Claude account (Pro / Max / Team / Enterprise).
- Free to use through 2026-06-22, then about half the price of the earlier preview version.
- If someone asks a clearly high-risk security or biology question, the system quietly routes the conversation to a more cautious model (Opus 4.8). Anthropic says this happens in fewer than 5% of conversations.

For two months, researchers warned that "this capability will eventually leak out." Yesterday Anthropic made the leak official — on purpose.

For most enterprise teams, the question just changed shape:

It used to be "Will our adversaries eventually get this capability?"

Today it is "Our own employees got it on day one. How do we see what they do with it before someone audits us?"

## The one-line takeaway

Anthropic also offers a free **Compliance API** — basically a built-in oversight hook that lets a company's security tools (the systems that track logins, scan messages, and flag policy violations) read what employees do with Claude. It's wired into 28 major security platforms.

It costs nothing. That's the point. It was designed to remove the security team's veto on adopting AI.

With Fable 5 now in every paid seat, that quiet oversight hook just stopped being a "nice to have" and started being the only thing separating "employees use AI in the open" from "employees use AI in the shadows."

## What integration companies should focus on

Three things matter, in this order:

1. **Model usage cost.** It's billed by the word, like a water meter. Help customers model what their bill will look like in July, before it arrives.
2. **Turning on the oversight properly.** Configure the Compliance API, set red-line policies, route the logs to the right places.
3. **Plug the logs into the security dashboards customers already have.** SIEM routing, alert correlation, audit trail integrity.

# 中文版

昨天（2026-06-09），Anthropic 公開上線 **Claude Fable 5**——這是第一款讓一般人也能用、具備同等「工業化找漏洞」能力的模型。兩個月前，同樣的能力還鎖在「防禦專用」的邀請名單後面。

## 實際上發生了什麼

- 過去鎖起來、會自動找出軟體漏洞的能力，現在出現在每一個付費版 Claude 帳號裡（Pro / Max / Team / Enterprise）
- 6/22 之前免費包含，之後計價約是先前 Preview 版的一半
- 如果有人問明顯高風險的資安或生物問題，系統會悄悄把對話轉接到比較保守的模型（Opus 4.8）。官方說這個觸發率小於 5%

過去兩個月，學界與研究單位一直警告「這個能力遲早會擴散」。昨天，Anthropic 自己把「擴散」這件事正式做了——而且是刻意的。

對大多數企業團隊，問題的形狀變了：

過去是「對手哪天會拿到這個能力」。

今天變成「我們自己員工第一天就拿到了。在被稽核之前，我們怎麼看見他們拿它做了什麼？」

## 一句話總結

Anthropic 同步提供一個 **免費的 Compliance API**——其實就是「內建的監看鉤子」，讓公司現有的資安工具（那些追蹤登入、掃描訊息、攔截違規的系統）可以讀到員工在 Claude 上的活動。它已經串好 28 家主流資安平台。

它免費，是有目的的。設計初衷是「替 AI 採購清掉資安團隊的否決票」。

Fable 5 鋪到每個付費席次的同一天，這個監看鉤子，正式從「有也不錯」變成「員工正大光明用 AI」與「員工偷偷用 AI」之間唯一的分界。

## 對系統整合公司，三件事最重要

順序也很重要：

1. **模型的用量成本。** 它按字數計費，像水電帳單。幫客戶在 7 月帳單到之前先做成本建模
2. **把監看打開來用。** 配置 Compliance API、設紅線政策、把紀錄送到對的地方
3. **把紀錄導進客戶現有的資安儀表板。** SIEM 路由、警報關聯、稽核軌跡完整性
