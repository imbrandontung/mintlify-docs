# English Version

We've covered the moment Fable 5 went public (Part 1) and how a free tool still makes money (Part 2). This one answers the cleanest question: why did Anthropic give the oversight tool away?

## Three reasons, in order

### ① To get past the security team

In most companies, the security and compliance team is the biggest hurdle for adopting any new AI. If you walk into a sales conversation and the oversight is already built in and free, you don't need to spend six months arguing with a skeptical security review.

The free hook removes a "no" from the procurement process. That alone is worth a lot.

### ② Free razor, paid blades

Since April 2026, Anthropic charges for usage and for seat licenses separately. The seat is small; the usage is where the real revenue is.

A free oversight layer encourages wider, deeper adoption. Wider, deeper adoption means more usage. More usage means more revenue.

Charging for the oversight tool would slow that — which is exactly the opposite of what Anthropic wants.

### ③ Building dependency, quietly

Twenty-eight security vendors have now built and pay to maintain integrations into the hook. Every additional integration makes Claude harder to remove from the customer's security setup — because removing Claude would also break those integrations.

Over time, the oversight tool quietly becomes the "shared dashboard for governing AI" inside the company. That's the strategic prize.

## The cynical reading isn't the whole story

Everything above — "it's a sales tactic" — is true. It is also incomplete.

The other side: **oversight is also a real responsibility, not just marketing.** Security leaders have a legal and professional duty to monitor what employees do with company tools. Regulators are paying attention. Auditable, controllable, boundary-respecting tools answer real obligations.

If you frame oversight as "lowering organizational risk," the sales outcome follows naturally. If you frame it as a sales hook, it ages badly. The order matters.

Both readings have to coexist — that's the honest narrative.

## Open ecosystem vs walled garden

Two philosophies are visible in the market right now:

- **Open ecosystem.** Publish a specification, invite security vendors to integrate at their own pace. You give up direct control of the experience, but you get deployment speed and vendor neutrality. This is Anthropic's approach.
- **Walled garden.** Bundle the oversight into your own productivity suite (e.g. Microsoft bundles AI oversight into its Purview tool, which comes with Microsoft 365). You keep full control of the experience, but third-party reach is slower, and the customer becomes more locked into your ecosystem.

Neither is wrong. They are bets on different futures:

- If AI oversight becomes a horizontal capability used across many AI models from many vendors → the open approach wins
- If AI oversight bundles into one or two dominant office suites → the walled garden wins

**Honest middle:** most large enterprises will run both. Plan for a hybrid setup, not an exclusive bet.

## For integrators

This is a positioning question. Pick a side. Advise your customers from that vantage point.

Don't pretend to be neutral — real neutrality usually means you haven't decided what you actually believe.

# 中文版

Part 1 講了 Fable 5 公開上線的時刻，Part 2 拆解免費工具怎麼賺錢。這篇回答最乾淨的一題：Anthropic 為什麼要把監看工具免費送出去？

## 三個原因，順序很重要

### ① 為了繞過資安團隊那一關

在大多數公司，資安與合規團隊是任何 AI 採購的最大阻力。如果你走進銷售會議，監看已經內建而且免費，你就不必花六個月對抗一場敵意的資安審查。

免費鉤子直接從採購流程裡拔掉一張「不行」。光這點就值很多錢。

### ② 免費的刀架，付費的刀片

2026/4 起，Anthropic 對「用量」和「席次費」分開計價。席次費小，用量才是真正的營收。

免費的監看層鼓勵更廣、更深的採用。更廣更深 → 更多用量 → 更多營收。

對監看收費會拖慢這個飛輪——正好是 Anthropic 最不想要的。

### ③ 安靜地建立依賴關係

28 家資安廠商已經自費建好、並付費維護自己的整合。每多一家整合，Claude 就更難從客戶的資安設定裡被拔掉——因為拔掉 Claude 同時會弄壞那些整合。

時間一久，這個監看工具就安靜變成「公司內部管理 AI 的共用儀表板」。這才是真正的戰略獎品。

## 這個 cynical 讀法不是故事全貌

上面三點——「這是銷售手段」——是真的。也是不完整的。

另一面是：**監看也是真實的責任，不只是行銷。** 資安主管有法律與專業上的義務，去監看員工拿公司工具做了什麼。監管機關也在看。可稽核、可控、尊重邊界的工具，回應的是真實的責任。

把監看定位成「降低組織風險」，銷售結果自然會發生。當銷售手段賣，過時得很快。順序很重要。

兩種讀法必須並存——這才是誠實的敘事。

## 開放生態 vs 封閉花園

市場上現在能看到兩種哲學：

- **開放生態。** 公布規格，讓資安廠商按自己節奏整合。放棄直接控制體驗，換取鋪設速度和廠商中立。這就是 Anthropic 的路線
- **封閉花園。** 把監看綁進自家生產力套件（例如微軟把 AI 監看綁進 Purview，跟 Microsoft 365 一起賣）。對體驗極致控制，但第三方擴散較慢，客戶也更難離開你的生態

哪個都不算錯。它們是對不同未來的下注：

- 若 AI 監看變成跨多家模型、跨多家廠商的水平能力 → 開放路線勝
- 若 AI 監看綁進一兩家主導的辦公套件 → 封閉花園勝

**誠實的中間值：** 大部分大型企業會兩種都跑。規劃混合方案，不要押單邊。

## 對整合商

這是「定位」問題。選一邊站，從那個視角給客戶建議。

別假裝中立——真正的中立通常意味著你還沒決定「自己到底相信什麼」。
